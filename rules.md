# Labeling rules

Written while hand-labeling 30 comments, before seeing any AI output. Each rule comes from a comment I couldn't classify on the first try.

1. Asks for a video, even if phrased as a question ("can you do a video on...?") → request
2. Implicit need / knowledge gap ("I'm clueless about X") → request
3. Offers services or collaboration to the creator → spam
4. Promotes a book or product through a made-up personal story → spam
5. Genuine thanks or support, even generic ("commenting for the algorithm") → praise
6. Praise combined with critique or advice → opinion (content outweighs thanks)
7. Negative or hostile criticism of the creator → opinion, not spam
8. Short take on a book, person or idea from the video ("MJ DeMarco is the real deal") → opinion
9. Emoji-only positive comment → praise
